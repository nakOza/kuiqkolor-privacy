What to include in a support request

  • device model;
  • Android or iOS version;
  • KuiqKolor app version;
  • a short description of the issue;
  • a screenshot when it helps explain a visual problem.

Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Common questions

Does KuiqKolor require an account?

No. The current mobile app works without a KuiqKolor account.

Where are saved colors and palettes stored?

They are stored locally on your device.

Does KuiqKolor require internet access?

The current core generator, preset, save, copy, and sharing workflow does not require a KuiqKolor online account or backend.

How does Share work?

KuiqKolor generates a palette image and opens your device's native share interface. You choose where it goes.
