Mobile Privacy Policy

Effective: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices of KuiqKolor Mobile. The current app is designed to function without an account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a KuiqKolor online backend.

No account required No ads No analytics Local saved data

2. Data KuiqKolor collects

The current version of KuiqKolor Mobile does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user data to the developer.

The app does not include a KuiqKolor account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, behavioral tracking, remote profile, or cloud-sync service.

3. Data stored locally on your device

KuiqKolor stores app preferences and user-created app state locally on your device so the app can remember your choices. This can include:

  • saved colors and saved palettes;
  • color lock state;
  • selected preset category;
  • Saved-page state and other local interface preferences.

This local data is not uploaded to a KuiqKolor server.

4. Clipboard

When you explicitly choose to copy a HEX color value, KuiqKolor writes that selected value to the operating system clipboard. The current app does not read unrelated clipboard contents or transmit clipboard data to the developer.

5. Palette sharing

When you explicitly choose Share, KuiqKolor generates a temporary palette image and passes it to the operating system's native share interface. KuiqKolor does not upload that image to a KuiqKolor server.

If you select another app or online service from the system share interface, that destination is governed by its own privacy practices.

6. Third-party software components

The current Flutter build uses software packages for local preferences, temporary file access, cross-platform file handling, and the operating system share interface. The current integration does not include advertising, analytics, or a remote KuiqKolor data service.

Store privacy disclosures are reviewed against the final release dependencies before submission.

7. Data retention and deletion

Saved colors, palettes, and preferences remain on your device until you remove them in the app, clear the app's local storage, or uninstall the app. Temporary share images are written to the operating system's temporary storage and may be removed by the operating system.

Because the current app does not create a KuiqKolor account or upload personal data to a KuiqKolor server, the developer does not maintain a remote user-data record to delete.

8. Consent and privacy choices

KuiqKolor does not currently perform developer-controlled personal-data collection that requires an in-app data-collection consent flow. You control local app data through the app, your device's app-storage controls, and uninstalling the app.

For privacy questions, contact nakOza@live.com.

9. Children's privacy

KuiqKolor is a general-purpose color and design utility and is not specifically directed to children.

10. Security

KuiqKolor minimizes privacy risk by keeping its saved color and preference data local and by not operating a remote user account or analytics backend in the current release.

11. Changes to this policy

If KuiqKolor's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the new behavior. The effective date at the top of the policy will also be updated when appropriate.
